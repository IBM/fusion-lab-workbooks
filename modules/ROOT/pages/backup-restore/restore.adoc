= Spoke Cluster Restore
:navtitle: Spoke Cluster Restore

== Introduction

In a Hub-and-Spoke topology, a single Fusion Hub cluster manages backups for applications running on multiple Spoke clusters. In this lab you will connect a second TechZone cluster as a Spoke, back up an application on the Hub using the `object1` location created in xref:backup-restore/locations-policies.adoc[Locations & Policies], and restore it to the Spoke.

include::partial$prereqs-techzone.adoc[]

[IMPORTANT]
====
This lab requires two TechZone IBM Fusion environments. The cluster with the full Backup & Restore service installed is the **Hub**. The second cluster is the **Spoke**. Keep both browser sessions open — you will switch between them throughout this lab.
====

== Install the Backup & Restore Agent on the Spoke

=== Step 1: Generate the Connection Snippet on the Hub

1. In the Fusion Console on the **Hub** cluster, navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Topology].
2. Click btn:[Connect cluster].
3. Click the **Use Fusion UI** tile.
4. Click btn:[Copy snippet] to copy the connection snippet to your clipboard.

=== Step 2: Install the Agent on the Spoke

1. In the Fusion Console on the **Spoke** cluster, navigate to menu:Services[].
2. Click the **Backup & Restore Agent** tile.
3. Click btn:[Install].
4. Paste the connection snippet from Step 1 into the **Hub Connection Snippet** field.
5. Open the **Storage class** drop-down and select `ocs-storagecluster-ceph-rbd`.
6. Click btn:[Install].
7. Wait for the **Installing...** progress bar to complete and the service to report **Healthy**.

=== Step 3: Verify the Connection on the Hub

1. Return to the Fusion Console on the **Hub** cluster.
2. Navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Topology].
3. Confirm the Spoke cluster is listed as **Connected** and **Healthy**.

== Verify the Backup Location is Accessible

The `object1` backup location created in xref:backup-restore/locations-policies.adoc[Locations & Policies] uses the FDF NooBaa/MCG S3 route, which is accessible from both clusters over the TechZone network. No additional OBC is required.

Confirm the location is still **Connected**:

1. In the Fusion Console on the **Hub**, navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Locations].
2. Verify `object1` shows **Connected**.

[NOTE]
====
IBM Fusion 2.13 enforces TLS certificate validation for backup locations. If the `object1` location shows a certificate error, you may need to provide the NooBaa CA bundle. Retrieve it with:

[source,shell]
----
oc get cm noobaa-ca-bundle -n openshift-storage -o jsonpath='{.data.ca-bundle\.crt}'
----

Paste the output into the **CA Certificate** field when editing the location in the Fusion Console.
====

== Create the Hub-Spoke Backup Policy

A dedicated policy scoped to the `object1` location is used for hub-spoke operations. If you already have `weekly-backup` pointing to `object1`, you can reuse it and skip this section.

1. Navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Policies].
2. Click btn:[Add policy +].
3. Set:
   * **Policy name:** `hubspoke-backup`
   * **Frequency:** `Monthly`, **Day:** `1`
4. Select the **Object Storage** tile and select `object1`.
5. Specify a **Time window** and click btn:[Create policy].

=== Assign the hubspoke-backup Policy

1. In the Fusion Console on the **Hub**, navigate to menu:Applications[].
2. Select the `hubspoke` checkbox and click btn:[Assign backup policy].
3. Select `hubspoke-backup`. Leave **Back up now** enabled. Click btn:[Save].
4. Monitor menu:Backup & restore[Backed up applications] until `hubspoke` shows **Completed** before proceeding.

== Restore to the Spoke Cluster

1. In the Fusion Console on the **Hub**, navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Backed up applications].
2. Click `hubspoke` to open its details page.
3. Click btn:[Restore].
4. In the **Cluster destination** drop-down, select the Spoke cluster — the one that does *not* show the **This cluster** tag.
5. Select **Use same project** and click btn:[Next].
6. Select the most recent backup time and click btn:[Next].
7. Review the summary and click btn:[Restore]. Confirm with btn:[Restore].

=== Monitor the Restore Job

Navigate to menu:Backup & restore[Jobs > Restore tab] on the **Hub**. Click the `restore-hubspoke-<date>` job and monitor until **Completed**.

*From the CLI on the Hub:*

[source,shell]
----
oc get pods -n ibm-spectrum-fusion-ns -w
----

=== Verify on the Spoke Cluster

1. In the OpenShift Web Console on the **Spoke**, navigate to menu:Workloads[Pods] (project: `hubspoke`).
2. Wait for the pod to show `1/1 Ready`. If the route is not accessible, restart the pod:
+
[source,shell]
----
oc delete pod -l app=filebrowser -n hubspoke
----
+
[NOTE]
====
This is a known post-restore behaviour — the application starts correctly but the route may not respond until the pod is restarted.
====
3. Navigate to menu:Networking[Routes] (project: `hubspoke`) on the **Spoke** and open the filebrowser URL.
4. Log in with **Username:** `admin` / **Password:** `admin`.
5. Confirm the directories and files created on the Hub are present — the cross-cluster restore is complete.
